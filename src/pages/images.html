The five cubic COLIBRE boxes, which have side lengths ranging from 25 to 400 comoving Mpc. Colour shows total surface density (in 5 Mpc thick faces) at z = 0. The volumes available at high (m5), intermediate (m6), and low (m7) resolution are indicated.
