Interactive maps

This page contains links to interactive maps of the COLIBRE simulations. The interactive viewer appears as shown in the image on the left. Features include:

  • Use the buttons in the top-left corner or the mouse scroll wheel to zoom.
  • Click and drag to move around the map.
  • Use the icon in the top-right corner to switch between different properties.
  • The scale bar in the bottom-left corner shows the length scale for the current zoom level.
  • A colour bar in the bottom-right corner represents the property values.
