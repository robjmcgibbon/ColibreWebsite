Sonified videos

Sonified spiral sequence, where sounds are mapped to different processes within that galaxy. Star formation rate is represented by a harmonic sound that changes in timbre, a windy sound that increases in intensity indicates the energy outflow rate, and a ticking sound marks constant mass increments accreted by the black hole.
